# Design references

These sources inspire the vocabulary and separation of concerns. They do not
validate this project's thresholds, weights or numerical relationships.

- Marsella & Gratch (2009), EMA: appraisal and coping. [Paper](https://people.ict.usc.edu/~gratch/CSCI534/Readings/COGSYS-RS-EMOTION-2008-6.pdf)
- Ryan & Deci (2000), self-determination theory: relatedness, autonomy and competence. [Paper](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2000_RyanDeci_SDT.pdf)
- Laurenceau et al. (1998), intimacy as an interpersonal process. [Paper](https://affective-science.org/pubs/1998/LaurenFBPl1998.pdf)
- Bancroft & Janssen (2000), dual-control model. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10880822/)
- Basson (2001), responsive desire framework. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11224952/)

Adult-related state is a separate configurable subsystem, default off. Observed
engagement metrics are not consent or permission. No adult transcripts or images
are distributed with the demo.
